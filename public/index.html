Where club ideas find their people.

Submit a project idea, browse what everyone's building, and get matched onto a team. Developers apply, tech leads pick their crew, and admins keep it all running.

How it works

From idea to team in three steps.

1

Submit an idea

Post your project with the stack, the roles you need, and a difficulty. It lands on the board instantly.

2

Devs show interest

Members browse projects and hit "I'm interested" with a preferred role and a note on what they'd bring.

3

Leads build teams

Tech leads review applicants, accept the right people, assign roles, and mark the project team formed.
